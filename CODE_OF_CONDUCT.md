# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project expects respectful, direct, and technically honest collaboration.

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and tradeoffs in good faith
- critique code and design decisions, not people
- make assumptions explicit
- keep disagreement factual and constructive
- respect different experience levels and backgrounds

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior
- bad-faith argumentation, trolling, or deliberate disruption
- publishing private or security-sensitive information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and community interactions that violate these standards.

Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers through the repository hosting platform's private contact or moderation features.

For security issues, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of public discussion.
